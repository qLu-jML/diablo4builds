Build of Death

Infinistep · Blazing Abyss · Shadowform Warlock

— Cheat Sheet · Diablo IV Season 13 · Lord of Hatred —

You're a permanent shadow. Shadowform stacks make you stealthed, and stealth makes you immortal. Footfalls of the Waning World turns Evade into Nether Step so you stay invisible while Blazing Scream and Profane Sentinel melt the screen. Eye of Baal stacks your damage every Soul Shard cast and Night Terror multiplies it per Shadowform stack — the longer the fight, the harder you hit. Bind Mastermind as your Soul Shard so Recasts don't break stealth.

Order of Operations · the right way to gear up Get Footfalls of the Waning World first — without it the build doesn't function. Then chase Eye of Baal at Bartuc, then Night Terror at Andariel. Slot Codex aspects on rares as placeholders so you have the build's effects active even before the BiS uniques drop. Bind Mastermind as soon as the Soul Shard drops.

Runeword Rule Runewords need two sockets in the same item and CANNOT coexist with gems on that item. Only Helm, Chest, Pants, or 2-H Weapon can host them. Cir + Gar goes in the chest (consistent crit chance), Cem + Ceh goes on the 2-H weapon (wolves carry your auras). Don't mix gems into either of those — gems go everywhere else.
Soul Shard · Bind This One Bind the Mastermind Soul Shard to your Greater Demon — it summons Laalish and lets Recast Skills fire without breaking Stealth. While in Shadowform, Abyss Skills deal +30% damage and you gain +5% MS per stack. Alternative: Blasphemous Fragment (Recast = Hex; Hexed enemies take +20% from Abyss Demonology).

Per-Item Upgrade Workflow

Repeat for every piece you slot in. Each step is a vendor or crafter — don't skip ahead.

  1. Acquire Ancestral 800 ilvl item (or Unique). Look for ★ Greater Affixes on a priority stat above.
  2. Imprint Aspect at the Occultist (Codex unlock or extracted from a salvaged Legendary). Use the aspect listed for that slot in the table above.
  3. Add Socket at the Jeweler — slot the listed gem or runeword pair.
  4. Temper twice at the Blacksmith with the listed Worldly + Offensive/Defensive/Utility manuals until the right affix rolls.
  5. Enchant at the Occultist — re-roll the lowest-priority affix toward the next missing entry in the table.
  6. Masterwork 12× at the Blacksmith. The crit upgrades at 4 / 8 / 12 should land on the bolded affix in the table — reset and re-roll if they don't.

How to Obtain Each Piece

  • Footfalls of the Waning World (Boots) — required core unique. Best target farm: Bartuc, Lord of Chaos. Also drops from Helltide Mystery Chests at WT4+.
  • Eye of Baal (Ring) — targeted boss farm at Bartuc, Lord of Chaos (south of Ked Bardu, Tormented).
  • Night Terror (Amulet) — dedicated drop pool on Andariel at the Hanged Man's Hall. Use Stygian Stones for Tormented summons.
  • Warlock Helm Unique (Command Laalish ult) — Tormented endgame boss drop pool. Check the Tree of Whispers boss summons for the current rotation.
  • Legendary Chest / Pants / Gloves / Ring 2 / Weapon — any Ancestral 800 ilvl drop. Best farms: Helltides (Mystery Chests) and The Pit.
  • Legendary 2-H Wand / Staff — Whisper caches at the Tree, Helltides, or boss drops. Look for the highest base damage roll before slotting your aspect.
  • Aspects (Codex) — imprintable Codex aspects come from completing dungeons. Open the Codex of Power, find each aspect listed, run its dungeon once. The Codex version is always max roll.
  • Aspects (Legendary extracts) — if an aspect isn't in the Codex, find a legendary that has it and salvage it at the Occultist with "Extract Aspect."
  • Runes (Cir, Gar, Cem, Ceh) — drop from Nightmare Dungeons and from Kurast Undercity runs (set tribute = Runes). Cir + Gar = chest. Cem + Ceh = weapon (wolves).
  • Gems (Royal tier) — upgrade lower-tier gems at the Jeweler. Royal Ruby = +%Armor. Skull = +Life on Hit.
  • Tempering Manuals — drop from Helltides (Tortured Gifts of Mysteries), Whisper caches at the Tree, and World Bosses. Make sure you have the listed Worldly/Offensive/Defensive/Utility manual before tempering.
  • Masterworking Materials — The Pit. Higher tiers = more materials. Plan to push T31+ once gear is online.
  • Glyph XP — The Pit. Run difficulty close to your max clear time of ~3 minutes for fastest XP.
  • Soul Shards — bind Mastermind at the Soul Shard altar. Mastermind drops from Tormented bosses; check the Greater Demon altar for the binding ritual.
