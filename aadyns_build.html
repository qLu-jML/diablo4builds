Holy Light Aura · Dawnfire · Sundered Night

— Cheat Sheet · Diablo IV Season 13 · Lord of Hatred —

Walk through packs and let your aura do the killing. Holy Light Aura is the damage, Dawnfire turns it into scaling fire, Sundered Night drops Consecration when you cast an aura — and Consecration is a massive aura-potency multiplier. Stay in Arbiter Form as much as possible (Disciple Oath).

Order of Operations · the right way to gear up Get Dawnfire first (it's the build's identity). Next Sundered Night, then any Tormented uber-boss runs for the rest. Slot Codex aspects on rares as placeholders so you have the build's effects active even before BiS uniques drop.

Per-Item Upgrade Workflow

Repeat for every piece you slot in. Each step is a vendor or crafter — don't skip ahead.

  1. Acquire Ancestral 800 ilvl item (or Unique). Look for ★ Greater Affixes on a priority stat above.
  2. Imprint Aspect at the Occultist (Codex unlock or extracted from a salvaged Legendary). Use the aspect listed for that slot in the table above.
  3. Add Socket at the Jeweler — slot the listed gem or runeword pair.
  4. Temper twice at the Blacksmith with the listed Worldly + Offensive/Defensive/Utility manuals until the right affix rolls.
  5. Enchant at the Occultist — re-roll the lowest-priority affix toward the next missing entry in the table.
  6. Masterwork 12× at the Blacksmith. The crit upgrades at 4 / 8 / 12 should land on the bolded affix in the table — reset and re-roll if they don't.

Paragon Glyphs

Skill points and full board paths skipped on purpose — only the glyphs matter for this sheet. Level glyphs in The Pit; they unlock max radius + legendary bonus at level 46. Place each glyph on the listed board at the listed rotation.

Rush each board's Legendary Node first, then the Glyph socket. The two key legendary nodes are Preacher (aura damage multiplier) and Castle (armor → damage).

How to Obtain Each Piece

  • Dawnfire (Gloves) — highest drop chance from Varshan in the Malignant Burrow under the Tree of Whispers (Hawezar). Summon with Malignant Hearts.
  • Sundered Night (2H Axe) — drops from Grigoire the Galvanic Saint south of Ked Bardu, OR target-farm Bartuc, Lord of Chaos for higher rate.
  • Heir of Perdition (Helm) — Tormented endgame boss drops; check the Paladin Unique drop table at the Tree of Whispers boss summons.
  • Shroud of False Death (Chest) — Tormented boss drop (Andariel / Duriel tier). Stack Stygian Stones + summon mats.
  • March of the Stalwart Soul (Boots) — general world drop; can target-farm at any high-level Paladin-class Tormented boss.
  • Argent Veil (Ring) — Tormented Uber boss drop pool — best odds at Duriel / Andariel.
  • Legendary Pants / Amulet / Ring 1 — any Ancestral 800 ilvl drop. Best farm: Helltides (Mystery Chests) and The Pit.
  • Aspects (Codex) — imprintable Codex aspects come from completing dungeons. Open the Codex of Power, find each aspect listed, and run its dungeon once. The Codex version is always max roll.
  • Aspects (Legendary extracts) — if an aspect isn't in the Codex, find a legendary that has it and salvage it at the Occultist with "Extract Aspect."
  • Runes (Moni, Ceh) — drop from Nightmare Dungeons and from Kurast Undercity runs (set tribute = Runes). Ceh is a Ritual rune; Moni is the partner.
  • Gems (Royal tier) — upgrade lower-tier gems at the Jeweler. Royal Ruby = +%Armor. Skull = +Life on Hit.
  • Tempering Manuals — drop from Helltides (Tortured Gifts of Mysteries), Whisper caches at the Tree, and World Bosses. Make sure you have the listed Worldly/Offensive/Defensive/Utility manual before tempering.
  • Masterworking Materials — The Pit. Higher tiers = more materials. Plan to push T31+ once gear is online.
  • Glyph XP — The Pit. Run difficulty close to your max clear time of ~3 minutes for fastest XP.
