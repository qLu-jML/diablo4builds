— Optimized Cheat Sheet · Diablo IV Season 13 · Lord of Hatred —

You are an unkillable shadow god. Footfalls of the Waning World grants infinite Nether Step while maintaining permanent Stealth. Mastermind Soul Shard + Metamorphosis - Terror Demon lets you spam Abyss skills without ever breaking stealth. Night Terror doubles damage in stealth while Eye of Baal ramps with every Soul Shard cast. You are invisible, untouchable, and hit exponentially harder the longer fights last.

Order of Operations · the right way to gear up Get Footfalls of the Waning World first — without it the build doesn't function. Then chase Eye of Baal at Bartuc, Night Terror at Andariel, and the Harash's Shadow Talisman set. Slot Codex aspects on rares as placeholders. Bind Mastermind immediately.

Per-Item Upgrade Workflow

Repeat for every piece you slot in. Each step is a vendor or crafter — don't skip ahead.

  1. Acquire Ancestral 800 ilvl item (or Unique). Look for ★ Greater Affixes on a priority stat above.
  2. Imprint Aspect at the Occultist (Codex or extracted).
  3. Add Socket at the Jeweler — slot the listed gem or runeword pair.
  4. Temper twice at the Blacksmith with the listed manuals.
  5. Enchant at the Occultist — re-roll lowest priority affix.
  6. Masterwork 12× at the Blacksmith. Crit upgrades on bolded affixes.

  • Eye of Baal — Bartuc (Tormented).

  • Aspects — Codex dungeons or extract at Occultist.
