ODIN'S RAVENSTORM v11 – Paragon Bonus Edition

One-Button Lightning Storm Druid (21 points at Level 15)

Paragon 91 Bonus Applied • Extra 6 Points Permanent

Note for you: Because you have Paragon 91 on another character, you start with 21 skill points at level 15 instead of 15. This version allocates all 21 points at Respec #1 and adjusts every later checkpoint accordingly.

Build Identity

  • Core Skill: Lightning Storm -> Form: Human -> Omnibolt.
  • Playstyle: preload utility (Cyclone/Hurricane/Ravens), then hold Lightning Storm.
  • Goal: maximize channel uptime first, then maximize damage scaling.
  • Dependency Rule: no mandatory unique required for 1-70 progression.

Legal Tree Rules (Must Follow)

Branching Constraints
  • Lightning Storm Minor Pair A: choose Additional Strikes or Damage Bonus (not both).
  • Lightning Storm Minor Pair B: choose Movement Speed or Minimum Strikes (not both).
  • Pick Form (Human/Werewolf) before choosing a Major variation.
  • For this build: Additional Strikes + Movement Speed + Human + Omnibolt.

Talisman / Charm Priority

  1. Spirit sustain effects first (resource return, max resource, reduced drain).
  2. Storm/Lightning amplification second.
  3. Controlled-target and Vulnerable interaction bonuses third.
  4. Crit/Lucky Hit support fourth.
  5. Pure defense/mobility luxury after channel stability is solved.

Rotation (One-Button Doctrine)

  1. Open pull: Cyclone -> Hurricane -> Ravens.
  2. Hold Lightning Storm continuously.
  3. Use Bulwark only for danger/CC, then immediately resume channel.
  4. Cataclysm for elite packs and bosses.
  5. If Spirit dips, single Wind Shear tap then return to hold.
