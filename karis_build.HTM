Falling Swife Paladin
Build of Grok Awesomeness

Endgame Blessed Hammer + Falling Star · Disciple Oath / Arbiter Hammerdin

— Cheat Sheet · Diablo IV Season 13 · Lord of Hatred —

You are a swift holy comet. Disciple Oath keeps Arbiter of Justice form up near-permanently. Blessed Hammer orbits you like a Hammerdin whirlwind via Disciple's Halo. Falling Star dives reset cooldowns, stack Ascension, apply Vulnerable, and proc auto-hammers through Argent Veil. Spam Rally (Words of Sacrifice) to convert HP into Faith and Movement Speed — Defiance Aura + life-on-hit refunds the cost.

Skills · Oath & Point Allocation

Oath: Disciple — Disciple skills proc 4.5s of Arbiter of Justice, +50% Disciple damage in form, Wing Strikes empowered. Total budget: 83 skill points (Level 70+).

Skill Tree Priority · Spend Points in This Order

  1. Max the Blessed Hammer cluster first — base damage, cast speed, and the Disciple's Halo upgrade so hammers orbit you.
  2. Max Falling Star — extra charges, Starfall eruptions, Vulnerable on landing, cooldown refund on vuln kills.
  3. Max Rally (Words of Sacrifice) — extra charges + MS; this is your basic skill spam.
  4. Arbiter of Justice cluster — Seraph's Wings, Wing Strike recast, Movement Speed in form. The whole build hinges on Arbiter uptime.
  5. Defiance Aura + Condemn — sustain + Vulnerable application. Don't max these until 1–4 are done.
  6. Key Passives: Holy Punishment, Ascension (stacks in Arbiter — huge damage ramp), Celestial Strife synergies.
Rotation · Brain-Dead Powerful Once Geared 1) Pop Defiance + Fanaticism Aura for buffs and Resolve.   2) Spam Rally for instant Faith + MS (HP cost is healed back).   3) Hold/spam Blessed Hammer — the orbiting whirlwind does the work.   4) Falling Star dive into packs constantly — refreshes Arbiter, drops Starfall, applies Vulnerable, stacks Ascension, procs auto-hammers.   5) Condemn to pull + stun + vuln.   6) In Arbiter form (~100% uptime), empowered Evade / Falling Star = insane mobility + extra hammer procs.
Order of Operations · the right way to gear up Get Herald's Morningstar first — without it the build doesn't scale. Then chase Argent Veil for auto-hammer dives. Build the Light's Epiphany 5-piece set in parallel from Whisper caches. Slot Codex aspects on rares as placeholders so the build's effects are active even before BiS uniques drop.

Runeword Rule Runewords need two sockets in the same item and CANNOT coexist with gems on that item. Only Helm, Chest, Pants, or Two-Handed Weapons can host them. Cir + Gar goes on the chest (consistent crit chance), Bac + Ohm goes on the pants (mobility + damage). Gems go everywhere else.

Per-Item Upgrade Workflow

Repeat for every piece you slot in. Each step is a vendor or crafter — don't skip ahead.

  1. Acquire Ancestral 800 ilvl item (or Unique). Look for ★ Greater Affixes on a priority stat above.
  2. Imprint Aspect at the Occultist (Codex unlock or extracted from a salvaged Legendary). Use the aspect listed for that slot in the table above.
  3. Add Socket at the Jeweler — slot the listed gem or runeword pair.
  4. Temper twice at the Blacksmith with the listed Worldly + Offensive/Defensive/Utility manuals until the right affix rolls.
  5. Enchant at the Occultist — re-roll the lowest-priority affix toward the next missing entry in the table.
  6. Masterwork 12× at the Blacksmith. The crit upgrades at 4 / 8 / 12 should land on the bolded affix in the table — reset and re-roll if they don't.

How to Obtain Each Piece

  • Herald's Morningstar (1H Mace) — required core unique. Target-farm Tormented bosses in the Paladin loot pool — best odds at Lord Zir or Bartuc.
  • Argent Veil (Ring) — Tormented Uber boss drop pool — best odds at Duriel / Andariel. Stack Stygian Stones + summon mats.
  • Light's Epiphany (5-piece Charm Set) — Talisman / Charm slot drops from Whispers caches at the Tree of Whispers and from Helltide Mystery Chests. Collect all 5 + a Unique 6th.
  • Mythic Harlequin Crest / Tyrael's Might — rare drop from any Tormented endgame boss. Best per-hour: pushed-tier The Pit bosses.
  • Tyrael's Jurisdiction / Herald of Zakarum (Shield) — Tormented Paladin boss drops; check the current rotation at the Tree of Whispers.
  • Legendary Helm / Chest / Pants / Gloves / Boots / Amulet / Ring 2 — any Ancestral 800 ilvl drop. Best farms: Helltides (Mystery Chests) and The Pit.
  • Aspects (Codex) — imprintable aspects come from completing dungeons. Run each dungeon once — Codex aspects always roll max.
  • Aspects (Legendary extracts) — if an aspect isn't in the Codex, find a legendary with it and salvage at the Occultist (Extract Aspect).
  • Runes (Cir, Gar, Bac, Ohm) — drop from Nightmare Dungeons and from Kurast Undercity runs (set tribute = Runes). Cir + Gar goes on chest, Bac + Ohm on pants.
  • Gems (Grand tier) — upgrade lower-tier gems at the Jeweler. Grand Ruby = +Strength / +Holy Dmg on weapon. Grand Diamond = +All Resist (jewelry).
  • Tempering Manuals — drop from Helltides (Tortured Gifts of Mysteries), Whisper caches, World Bosses. Have the right manual before tempering.
  • Masterworking Materials — The Pit. Higher tiers = more materials. Plan to push T31+ once gear is online.
  • Glyph XP — The Pit. Run difficulty close to your max ~3-minute clear for fastest XP.
  • Mercenaries — recruit Raheir (tank/support) + Varyana (damage) at the Mercenary Hall.
