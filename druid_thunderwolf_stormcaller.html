THUNDERWOLF STORMCALLER

Lightning Storm Druid • One-Button Leveling Build • Season 13

Playstyle — Hold Lightning Storm ⚡

Core Loop: Hold Lightning Storm to channel massive AoE lightning. Omnibolt merges strikes for huge single-target damage. Wind Shear as filler early. Everything else is passive or long cooldown.

This is one of the fastest and most powerful lightning-themed leveling builds. Pure storm visuals, excellent clear speed, and smooth scaling to 70+.
