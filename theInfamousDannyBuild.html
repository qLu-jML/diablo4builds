Incinerate and Chill Mode

Flamethrower Turret · Fire + Frost Multi-Element · Stationary Inferno

— Cheat Sheet · Diablo IV Season 13 · Lord of Hatred —

Become an unmovable Incinerate turret. Channel Incinerate (Flamethrower + Overheating ramp) to shotgun packs and bosses with triple beams. Drop Inferno (Supreme) for grouping, mana-free Pyromancy, and massive buffs. Frost (Ice Armor + Frost Nova) provides unbreakable barriers, CC, and Tal Rasha / Storm Swell multipliers. Stand still, chill, and melt everything.

Order of Operations · Gear Up This Way 1. Unlock Flamethrower’s Aspect + Aspect of Overheating.
2. Farm Tal Rasha’s Iridescent Loop + Ring of Starless Skies.
3. Prioritize Harlequin Crest, Shroud of False Death, and defensive Uniques.
Use Codex aspects on rares immediately for full functionality.
